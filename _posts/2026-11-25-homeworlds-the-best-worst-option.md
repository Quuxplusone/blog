## The puzzle

![A graphical representation of the game state.](/blog/images/2026-11-25-the-best-worst-option.png)

    Lee (0,y3) r2y1g1-y3
    Ray (1,g3) y1-r3g1g1b1
    DS1 (b1) r1b2-
    DS2 (b1) -r1r1y1

(The stash contains `r3r3r2r2 y3y2y2y2 g3g3g2g2g2 b3b3b3b2b2`.)

Lee has almost lost this game of Homeworlds: Ray is threatening to win by any of "sac r3,"
"move y1," or "sac g1"; and if Ray wins, naturally Lee loses.

What is Lee's best move at this point?
